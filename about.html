Plan Your Day at the Festival

Everything you need to know for your visit

Our International Food Festival is a joyful community celebration with delicious cuisine, warm hospitality, and fun for all ages. Come experience the rich flavors and traditions shared by our parish family while supporting the ongoing ministries of St. Anthony.

Whether you are visiting for your favorite dish, exploring something new, or simply spending time with friends and family, this festival is a beautiful way to connect with the community and enjoy a weekend full of culture, faith, and fellowship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is parking free?

Yes! Main festival parking is available in the festival parking lot, with entrance access from Alvin A. Klein Drive. The main church lot off FM 2920 is reserved and includes designated handicapped spaces.

Can I bring my dog?

Service animals are permitted. Please leave pets at home due to food service areas and large crowds.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Proper clothing and footwear are required throughout the festival grounds. Appropriate coverage must be maintained, and clothing with offensive language or imagery is not permitted. As this festival is hosted by our church, we kindly ask all guests to wear attire that is suitable for a church setting.

Are there vegetarian options?

Absolutely! Look for (V) markers on menu items.

Is the festival rain or shine?

Yes! The festival includes both indoor and outdoor activities. Vendors and select church activities will be located inside the fellowship hall, providing a comfortable indoor space.

Is there a Lost and Found?

Please email info@satginternationalfoodfest.org for any lost and found items.

Are handguns allowed on the property?

Firearms are prohibited on this property pursuant to Texas Penal Code §§ 30.06 and 30.07, which restrict both concealed carry and open carry.

Is the festival family-friendly?

Yes! Bring the whole family and enjoy a variety of fun activities for the kids at our Children's Village. Our Children’s Village is an exclusive event on Saturday, October 10 from 11 AM-6 PM.
